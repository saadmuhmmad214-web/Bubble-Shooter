How to Play

1

Drag anywhere to aim. A dashed line shows where the bubble will go, including wall bounces.

2

Release to fire. The bubble sticks to the first spot it touches.

3

Match 3 or more of the same color to pop them. Bubbles that lose their anchor fall too!

4

Tap the shooter to swap the current bubble with the next one.

5

Use a Bomb to blow up all nearby bubbles. You have a limited number per level.

6

Watch the red danger line. If bubbles cross it, the level ends.
